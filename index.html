Privacy Policy — EasyDont

Last updated: 2026-10-02

In short

EasyDont is a client for Mastodon, the decentralised social network. It talks directly to the Mastodon server you choose and does not go through any server of ours. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts with the developer. We do not sell or share data with anyone. Your posts, messages and account data live on your Mastodon server and are governed by that server's own privacy policy.

EasyDont is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Mastodon gGmbH or the Mastodon project.

Data controller and contact

Luca Colombo, Italy. For any privacy matter write to luca.colombo@ik.me.

What the app does on your device

  • Sign-in. You sign in through the OAuth page of your own Mastodon server, in the browser. The app never sees your password. The access token is kept in the operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Signing out revokes the token on the server.
  • Local data. Settings, reading positions, saved filters, drafts and the data-usage counters are stored only on your device. Drafts are encrypted. Android backups of the app are disabled, so this data is not copied to your Google account.
  • App lock. If you enable it, the fingerprint/PIN check is done by the operating system; the app receives only a yes/no answer.
  • Permissions. Internet (to reach Mastodon servers), notifications, and biometrics (only for the app lock).

Connections the app makes

Using the app necessarily sends your requests (and your IP address) to the following parties. We do not receive any of it.

  • Your Mastodon server(s) — everything you read, write or change. Their operators are responsible for that data.
  • Other Mastodon servers — to show complete lists of followers, boosts, favourites or replies, the app may query the public API of the server where an account or post originates. These requests carry no token and no account information.
  • joinmastodon.org — only if you use "Explore servers" to look for servers with open registration.
  • Link and media hosts — images, videos and link previews are loaded from the addresses contained in posts.

Translation of posts

You choose how posts are translated (Settings → Translation). Depending on your choice:

  • Mastodon server. The text of the post is sent to your server's translation service, which may be run by a third party chosen by the server's operator.
  • TranslatePsy (on the device). Translation runs offline on your phone. The language models are not bundled: they are downloaded from Hugging Face only after you consent, and the download server sees your IP address. Updates are searched for only on request.
  • Google ML Kit (on the device). Translation and the optional text recognition for alt text run on the phone, but Google's ML Kit downloads its models from Google servers and sends Google diagnostic data (e.g. device model, installation identifier, languages), as described in Google's "ML Kit data disclosure". For this reason this option is not tracking-free.

Notifications

  • Push (UnifiedPush). Mastodon sends the notification through the push distributor you use. With the built-in distributor on devices with Google Play services, the message travels through Google's push infrastructure, which sees that a notification exists, not its readable content (it is encrypted end to end between your server and the app).
  • Without Google Play services the app checks your server periodically (about every 15 minutes) in the background.

Feedback form (optional)

If you send feedback from Settings, the message (max. 1000 characters), the app version and, only if you enter it, your email address and any screenshots you attach are sent to a relay run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards them to the developer by email.

  • Your IP address is not stored; it is used only for a rate limit and discarded after 2 minutes.
  • A message without attachments is kept in a queue for at most 7 days until the daily summary is sent, then deleted. A fingerprint (SHA-256) of the message is kept for 24 hours to discard duplicates. Screenshots are not stored on the relay.
  • An email address, if given, is used only to reply to you.
  • Like any email, the message passes through the mail systems involved.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending the form.

Optional "Support the project" link

The app can open a Ko-fi page in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you tap the link; Ko-fi then applies its own privacy policy.

Widget and sharing

The Android home-screen widget shows data already stored on your device. When you share text, links or images to EasyDont from another app, the content is used only to compose a post that you review before sending.

App stores

If you installed the app from Google Play or the App Store, these stores may collect their own data under their own policies: Google, Apple.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability (Articles 15–22). Because the developer does not keep your data beyond what is described above, there is usually nothing to act on; you can still write to the address above. For data on your Mastodon server, contact that server's operator. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Mastodon servers set their own minimum age.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Feedback data is retained as described above. To remove your Mastodon data, delete it from your server or delete your account there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and, for significant changes, the app's "What's new" screen mentions it.
